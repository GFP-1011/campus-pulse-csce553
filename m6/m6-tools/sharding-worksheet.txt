Step 3 · Check for a hot owner
Hashing spreads many owners across shards, but it cannot split the writes of one owner. Name your worst case.
* The busiest single owner we can imagine (a celebrity account, a sold-out event, a reseller bot): ______________________________
* What it writes, how often: ______________________________
* Which single row gets locked by every one of those writes (a counter, a seat count): ______________________________
* Our plan: ☐ Insert rows instead of updating one counter ☐ Queue or rate-limit the burst ☐ Accept it and say why: ______________
Hint (Capacity = the busiest shard): sharding helps many busy owners at once, not one very busy owner.
